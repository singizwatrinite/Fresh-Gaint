ABOUT FRESH LINK

Farm Fresh. Direct Delivery

Why we exist

At Fresh Link, we believe food shouldn't lose its freshness on the way to your kitchen.

Who we are

We are a direct farm-to-consumer grocery delivery service on a mission to empower local farmers and deliver unmatched freshness to customers, wherever they are. By sourcing directly from village farms, we ensure that farmers receive fair prices for their labor, while you receive produce that is harvested—not days or weeks later in a cold storage warehouse.

From the moment a vegetable is picked to the moment it arrives at your door, we handle every step with care, transparency, and a deep respect for the land and the people who tend it.

We aren’t just delivering groceries. We are delivering dignity to farmers, health to consumers, and sustainability to our planet.
