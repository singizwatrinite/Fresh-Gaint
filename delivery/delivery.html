Delivery terms

Where we deliver, when to expect it, and how your order is handled along the way.

The essentials

Delivery area All of Kigali (KGL) — free on every order, no minimum spend
Order cutoff Orders placed before early afternoon are picked the same day
Typical delivery window Same day, within a few hours of picking, along a fixed route
Payment on delivery Cash on Delivery, Mobile Money, or Card at checkout
If you're not home We call the number on your order — we don't leave perishables unattended

How your order is handled

Produce is boxed by category the same morning it's picked, with anything perishable packed last so it spends the least time possible outside a cooler. Your delivery partner runs a fixed route across Kigali, which keeps arrival windows predictable rather than "sometime today."

Missing landmarks or gate numbers are the most common cause of delays — double-check your address details at checkout to keep things moving smoothly.
