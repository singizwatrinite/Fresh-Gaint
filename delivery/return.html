Returns & refunds

Fresh groceries need a different return policy than a t-shirt — here's how we handle it fairly.

What's covered

  • Anything that arrives damaged, spoiled, or clearly not fresh — report it within 24 hours of delivery for a replacement or full refund.
  • Wrong items delivered — we'll bring the correct product and collect the mistake at no extra cost.
  • Missing items from your order — refunded to your original payment method or credited to your next order, your choice.

What isn't covered

  • Change-of-mind returns on perishable produce once it's left our sorting table — we can't resell fresh food that's been in someone else's kitchen.
  • Items reported more than 24 hours after delivery, since freshness can no longer be verified.
  • Normal ripening or minor cosmetic variation in fruits and vegetables — that's just how real produce looks.

How to request one

Call or message us with your order number and a quick photo of the issue if it's damage-related. Most requests are resolved the same day — either a replacement goes out on the next available route, or a refund is issued straight to how you paid.

Call or WhatsApp 07888888, or message @freshlink on Instagram.
