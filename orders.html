How to make an order

Five steps, start to doorstep — here's exactly what happens when you shop with Fruitiva.

  1. Browse the shop

    Head to the Shop page and filter by category, or jump straight to Recommended or Special Offer from the home page. Click any product to view full details before adding it.

  2. Add items to your cart

    Tap "Add to Cart" on as many products as you need. Your cart total updates instantly in the top bar, so you always know where you stand before checkout.

  3. Review your cart

    Open the cart page to adjust quantities, remove anything you changed your mind about, and confirm your delivery address in Kigali.

  4. Choose how you'll pay

    At checkout, pick Cash on Delivery, Mobile Money, or Card. All three are handled on the same secure checkout flow — pick whichever is easiest for you.

  5. Track it to your door

    Once confirmed, your order is picked the same day and handed to a delivery partner running a fixed route across Kigali. If you're not reachable, we'll call the number on your order rather than leave it unattended.

Still stuck?

If anything about placing an order isn't working the way it should, our team can walk you through it directly rather than you troubleshooting alone.
